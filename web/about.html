ABOUT

Built by people who got frustrated.

Aura started as a personal problem. It became a project. Then a prototype. Then two.

In 2013, a film called Her imagined a world where AI was ambient — present, warm, and woven into daily life without demanding attention.

Ten years later, we have AI. We buried it in the wrong device.

Every interaction still requires a conscious act: unlock the phone, open an app, wait, read. The interface gets in the way of the thought.

Aura is our attempt to build what Her imagined. Not a product. A proof that it is possible.

Two semesters. Two prototypes. One necklace. Under $70.
