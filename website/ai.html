Audio Intelligence

Real-time listening with Deepgram

Aura captures every nuance of human speech using state-of-the-art Nova-2 models. With sub-300ms latency and high-fidelity word accuracy, the boundary between machine and conversation dissolves.

LPU
Inference Engine

Latency at the limit of physics

Powered by Groq's LPU technology, Aura processes language tokens at speeds that feel instantaneous. The typical delay of cloud-based AI is eliminated, replaced by a responsive fluidic exchange that keeps up with the fastest thinkers.

Multimodal Sight

See the world through GPT-4o

Aura doesn't just hear you; it understands context. Integration with GPT-4o vision capabilities allows for complex spatial reasoning and object recognition, making Aura a true partner in physical environments.

Long-term Memory

Persistence with Pinecone

Knowledge is preserved. Using Pinecone's serverless vector architecture, Aura maintains a semantic index of your interactions, ensuring that every session builds upon the last with perfect contextual recall.
