What Aura does not do yet

We believe in building in public, which means being honest about limitations.

  • No offline mode: If you lose internet, Aura stops working. Local STT/LLMs are too large for the ESP32 right now.
  • Video streaming: The ESP32-S3 cannot encode h.264 video. We send JPEG frames at 1fps instead.
  • Speaker output: The current hardware has no speaker. Responses are delivered to your phone or connected earbuds.
  • Wake word: Always-on listening drains the 400mAh battery in 2 hours. We currently use a physical tap-to-talk mechanism to ensure 12h battery life.
