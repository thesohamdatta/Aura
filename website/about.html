Built by people
who got frustrated.

In late 2025, we watched the tech industry pivot to wearable AI. Products launched with huge promises, closed ecosystems, and price tags north of $700. They required monthly subscriptions just to access your own memory.

We were undergraduate engineering students in Pune, India. We couldn't afford them. More importantly, we didn't want to rely on closed systems for something as intimate as our personal memory.

We realized the math didn't add up. The sensors required â€” an I2S microphone, a low-res camera, a bluetooth chip â€” were incredibly cheap. The real magic was happening in the cloud anyway.

So we built Aura. An open-source, $50 wearable that does exactly what the proprietary devices do, but you own the hardware, and you route the data to whichever LLM you prefer.
