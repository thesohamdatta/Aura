About Aura

We are four undergraduates from Pune, India, studying AI and Machine Learning at PES Modern College of Engineering. We built Aura with our own money, over nights and weekends, because we wanted to understand what it would feel like to carry AI — not in a phone, but around our necks.

The project started as a question: what if the interface disappeared? Phones demand your eyes. Laptops demand your desk. We wanted something that asked for neither.

Aura is built on the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense — a microcontroller smaller than a matchbox. The case is 3D printed. The battery lasts one to two hours. We state this plainly, not as a limitation but as a fact.

Prof. Sonal Pansare guided us through the hardware architecture and kept the project grounded in what was real, not what was wished for.

We built on the Omi open-source ecosystem — github.com/BasedHardware/omi — because it gave us a foundation to build from, not a ceiling to build under.

Aura is MIT licensed because it had to be. Open hardware, open firmware, open backend. If you can improve it, you should.
