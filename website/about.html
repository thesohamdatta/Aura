About

The journey of Aura began in a shared dorm room, driven by the belief that intelligence should be personal, private, and accessible to all.

Our Mission

To create less socially disruptive technology. To change the behaviors engineered by social media giants over the years, and to change how we live and use the internet.

Our Vision

Create and Operating System for the AI and Human connection .
