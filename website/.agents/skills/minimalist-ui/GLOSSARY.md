# Glossary — Minimalist UI

## Restraint

The discipline of leaving out. Every element, color, animation, and border must justify its existence. If it does not serve function or meaning, it is noise. Apple's design philosophy: "Simple can be harder than complex."

_Avoid_: minimalism, simplicity, clean

## Space

White space as structural element. Not emptiness — rhythm. Vertical padding (80px/60px) separates narrative sections. Gutters (24px) separate content. Max-widths (980px/1200px) constrain flow. Space is the invisible grid that makes content legible.

_Avoid_: padding, margin, whitespace, breathing room

## Less

The method of minimalist design. Start with everything, remove until it breaks, add back one. Each removal reveals what matters. The test: does this element earn its tokens?

_Avoid_: subtraction, reduction, stripping

## Calm

The emotional state of a well-designed minimalist interface. No visual noise, no competing focal points, no urgency. The user's eye moves naturally. Content is the protagonist. Apple's design goal: "The intersection of technology and the liberal arts."

_Avoid_: peaceful, serene, clean aesthetic

## Tonal Layering

Creating depth through background color changes, not shadows. White on parchment, parchment on white. The UI feels architectural — flat surfaces stacked by color, not elevation. Shadows are reserved for product renders only.

_Avoid_: elevation, depth, shadow hierarchy
