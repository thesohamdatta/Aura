# Skill: Aura Content & Copywriting

> Load this skill when writing or editing copy for the Aura website.

## When to Use

- Writing new section headlines or body copy
- Editing existing content
- Creating FAQ answers
- Writing meta descriptions

## Tone Guide

> "Certainty, not excitement." — The tone is a scientist who watched *Her* and felt something.

### Rules
1. **No exclamation marks.** Ever. Not one.
2. **Lead with the verb.** "Transcribes in 300ms" not "Aura features real-time transcription"
3. **One idea per sentence.** No compound sentences.
4. **Specific over general.** "Deepgram Nova-2" not "industry-leading STT"
5. **No adjectives unless measurable.** "Fast" → "<300ms". "Affordable" → "$50 BOM"
6. **Technical terms are fine.** The audience knows Pinecone, Groq, ESP32.
7. **No startup buzzwords.** No "game-changing", "revolutionary", "disruptive", "next-gen"
8. **No AI clichés.** No "powered by AI", "artificial intelligence", "machine learning"

## Content Patterns

### Headlines
- Short (2-5 words)
- Tight letter-spacing (-0.02em to -0.03em)
- Active voice
- Examples: "Worn. Screenless. Aware." / "The third device." / "Built by students. Owned by everyone."

### Subheadlines
- One sentence explaining the headline
- 16-17px, secondary color, line-height 1.7
- Examples: "An open-source AI pendant worn around the neck."

### Body Copy
- 16px, primary text color
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Technical details in `body-sm`, tertiary color

### CTAs
- Two options: primary (filled) + secondary (outlined)
- Verb-first: "Build Yours" / "View on GitHub" / "Read the paper"
- Never "Click here" or "Learn more"

### Section Labels
- 12px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.1em, accent color
- One or two words: "Core capabilities" / "How it works" / "Research"

## Existing Copy to Preserve
These headlines are final. Do not rewrite:
- "Worn. Screenless. Aware."
- "The third device."
- "Built by students. Owned by everyone."
- "Peer-reviewed, not hand-waved."
- "Three senses. One pendant."
- "Hear. Think. Remember."
- "Made in Pune."
- "Questions."

## FAQ Answer Style
- Start with a direct answer (first sentence)
- Provide technical detail (second sentence)
- End with a concrete fact (numbers, model names, capabilities)
- 2-3 sentences per answer maximum
